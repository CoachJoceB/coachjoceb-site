Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Legacy Lab Academy, Inc. ("we," "us," "Coach J") operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Payment information: When you purchase a membership or drop-in session, our payment processor Stripe collects your name, email address, and payment details. We do not store your card numbers. Stripe's privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Waiver information: When you complete the training waiver, we collect athlete and parent/guardian contact details, emergency contacts, and medical disclosures needed to run safe training sessions.
  • Communications: If you contact us, we keep your message so we can respond.

How We Use It

  • To book your training sessions and send confirmations
  • To keep athletes safe (emergency contacts, medical notes)
  • To process payments and manage memberships
  • To respond to your questions

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Sharing

We share limited data only with the services that run this site: Stripe (payments), Google (calendar scheduling and email), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each has its own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Our training serves minors. Waiver and registration information for athletes under 18 is provided by a parent or legal guardian, and we use it only to operate the training program.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by contacting us at the phone number listed on our main page. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version here with a new date.
