Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Monthly Membership

  • Cancel anytime. Cancellation stops future charges and takes effect at the end of your current billing month.
  • We do not prorate or refund partial months.
  • To cancel, use the Stripe customer portal link in your confirmation email or contact us directly and we will cancel it for you.

Drop-In Sessions

  • Full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before the session.
  • No refund for cancellations within 24 hours of the session or for no-shows.
  • If we cancel a session (weather, facility issue), you get a full refund or a credit toward a future session, your choice.

How Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Questions

Contact us at the phone number on our main page and we will sort it out.
