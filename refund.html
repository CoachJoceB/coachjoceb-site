Refund Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

No Refunds

All sales are final. We do not issue refunds for monthly memberships, drop-in sessions, or shooting sessions, including for missed sessions, late cancellations, or no-shows.

Rescheduling

  • If you cannot make a session, you may reschedule to another available session.
  • To reschedule, contact us at least 24 hours before your session using the phone number on our main page.
  • Rescheduled sessions are subject to availability. We will work with you to find the next open spot.

If We Cancel

If we cancel a session (weather, facility issue, or schedule change), you will be moved to the next available session at no extra charge.

Availability

Session times and availability are subject to change. We post the current schedule on our website and will notify booked athletes of any changes by email.

Monthly Membership Cancellation

You may cancel your monthly membership at any time through the Stripe customer portal or by contacting us. Cancellation stops future charges and takes effect at the end of your current billing month. We do not prorate or refund partial months.

Questions

Contact us at the phone number on our main page and we will sort it out.
