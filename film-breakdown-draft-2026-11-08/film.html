How It Works

Four steps, all online. Your only job is to send one full game.

1

Send Your Film

Pick a package below, then complete the short intake and paste your full game film link (NFHS Network, Hudl, YouTube, or Google Drive). Full games only, not highlight reels. Highlights hide the possessions that teach the most.

2

Coach J Breaks It Down

Coach J watches the full game and marks the plays that matter: reads, decisions, positioning, shot selection, and the moments that changed the game.

3

Meet Live to Review

A 30-minute video call with Coach J and the athlete. Walk the film together, pause anywhere, ask anything. A parent or guardian is on the invite for every athlete under 18.

4

Keep Everything

After the call you receive the full recording, a one-page report with 3 strengths and 3 growth areas, and drills matched to what your film showed.

What Coach J Looks At

The same things college coaches look at, explained in plain language an athlete can act on.

Decision Making

Reads with the ball, when to attack, when to move it, and how the defense was beaten or not.

Shot Selection and Mechanics

Which shots were the right shots, and the footwork and form behind the makes and misses.

Defense

On-ball position, closeouts, help and recovery, and effort plays that do not show in a box score.

Off-Ball Play

Spacing, cutting, screening, and relocation. Most of the game is played without the ball.

Transition and Pace

Sprint lanes, early offense, and how the game speeds up or slows down with you on the floor.

Presence

Body language, communication, and response to mistakes. Coaches notice all of it.

FAQs

Quick answers before you send your film.

What film should I send?
One full game: school, AAU, or league film where the athlete plays real minutes. Please do not send highlight reels. Highlights only show the good plays, and a breakdown is built on everything in between. An NFHS Network link, a Hudl link, an unlisted YouTube video, or a Google Drive share link all work.
How does scheduling work?
After checkout you complete the short intake form with your film link. We email review call times to the parent email within one business day, with evening and weekend options. Calls run 30 minutes on video.
Who is on the review call?
The athlete and Coach J, plus a parent or guardian on the invite for every athlete under 18. Parents are welcome to listen in and ask questions at the end.
What do we get after the call?
The full call recording, a one-page written report with 3 strengths and 3 growth areas, and drills matched to the film, all by email within 24 hours of the call.
Is this for boys and girls?
Yes. Film breakdown is open to middle school, high school, and AAU athletes, boys and girls, anywhere. Coach J trains and coaches both.
Will this help with recruiting?
This is player development, not a recruiting service. What it does is show an athlete exactly what coaches see, and give them a clear plan to fix it. That is the work that changes recruiting conversations later.
Can I cancel Film Room Monthly?
Yes, anytime through the customer portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle. See our refund policy for full details.
