What this program is

BecomingHER 1:1 mentorship pairs mindset training with strength and mobility fitness in an environment built for teen girls to feel mentally, physically, and emotionally confident and safe. One session per week, about one hour, focused on confidence, mindset, and leadership. Sessions may include conversation, reflection exercises, creative activities, and gentle movement such as walking, stretching, mobility work, or light bodyweight strength.

What this program is not: it is not medical care, mental-health therapy, nutritional counseling, or athletic conditioning. The mentor is not a medical provider and does not diagnose, treat, or advise on medical conditions, food, or eating.
