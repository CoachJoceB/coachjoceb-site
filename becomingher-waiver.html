What this program is

BecomingHER is a mentorship program for teen girls that mixes mindset training with fitness, in an environment built for girls to feel mentally, physically, and emotionally confident and safe. The first session is one-on-one; later sessions may be one-on-one or in a small group of girls in the program. Sessions run one per week, about one hour, focused on confidence, mindset, and leadership. Sessions may include conversation, reflection exercises, creative activities, and fitness work such as walking, stretching, mobility work, bodyweight exercises, and supervised use of weights and medicine balls. This is mindset and fitness training, not athletic conditioning.

What this program is not: it is not medical care, mental-health therapy, nutritional counseling, or athletic conditioning. The mentor is not a medical provider and does not diagnose, treat, or advise on medical conditions, food, or eating.
