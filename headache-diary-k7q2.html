Tips for parents and caregivers

From the Children's Hospital Boston headache diary

  • Pay attention to changes in the child's behavior just before, during, and after the headache. Track these changes in the diary.
  • Use the pain faces to help the child explain how much their head hurts. Show the child the faces when the headache starts, during the headache, and again after steps have been taken to ease the pain.
  • Remember to bring the headache diary to all healthcare appointments.
Based on the headache diary from the National Headache Foundation and Children's Hospital Boston. For more information about headache causes and treatments, visit headaches.org or call 312-274-2650.
