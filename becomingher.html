The program

What BecomingHER is

Confidence is not a personality trait. It is a rep. BecomingHER treats confidence like strength: you build it with small, repeated work, and you can watch it grow.

Every session pairs one clear mindset theme with real strength and mobility training. Girls train with dumbbells, medicine balls, slam balls, and resistance bands, always with good form first and loads that fit her age and experience. The mindset work happens while we move, taught as a conversation, not a lecture: self talk, bouncing back after a mistake, handling pressure, goal setting, and trusting her own voice.

This is mindset mixed with fitness, never athletic conditioning. No endless laps, no wind sprints, no punishment workouts.

Her first session is 1:1 with Coach J. After that, sessions may stay 1:1 or join a small group of four to six girls, so everyone is seen and no one hides in the back. Sessions run 45 minutes, once a week, at the same time each week, at 3920 W. Navy Blvd in Pensacola. Your exact day and time are confirmed when you book the free intro.

Inside a session

Same structure every week

Girls relax when they know what is coming. The structure stays the same so the work can go deeper.

  1. Check In0 to 5 min

    Rose, thorn, bud, or a simple 1 to 10 energy number. No pressure to share more than she wants.

  2. Warm Up and Move5 to 15 min

    Dynamic warm up, mobility work, and breathing drills that get her body ready to train and her head ready to focus.

  3. Train and Talk15 to 30 min

    Strength and mobility work with dumbbells, medicine balls, and bands while we talk through the mindset theme of the week.

  4. Whiteboard Work30 to 40 min

    We map the week's theme on the whiteboard, review goals in her training journal, and pick the focus for the days ahead.

  5. One Goal40 to 45 min

    We close with one small, specific goal for the week. Not homework. One rep to try in practice or in a game.

The founding pilot runs in a four week arc: Foundations, The Voice in My Head, Strong and Recovered, and Compete With Confidence. Girls can repeat it, because the reps get stronger every time around.
