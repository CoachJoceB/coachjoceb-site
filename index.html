FAQs

Quick answers before you hit the court.

How do I sign up?
Pick a plan above and check out securely with Stripe. After payment you will be sent to the training waiver (about five minutes). Already done a waiver? You are all set. You are automatically booked for Friday training and get a confirmation email.
When are sessions?
Fridays only. Fundamentals 4:00 PM - 4:45 PM, Intermediate/Advanced Group Training 5:30 PM - 6:30 PM, Intermediate/Advanced Shooting Training 6:30 PM - 7:30 PM. All sessions at 3920 W Navy Blvd, Pensacola, FL 32507.
What is included in monthly?
4 Intermediate/Advanced Group Training Sessions and 4 Intermediate/Advanced Shooting Sessions every month. Skill development, ball handling, finishing, shooting, game-speed decision making, competitive drills, plus dedicated shooting mechanics work.
Do you accept Step Up for Students?
Yes. Log in at stepupforstudents.org, search for Legacy Lab under providers, then complete the training waiver and select Step Up as your payment method.
What should my athlete bring?
Basketball shoes, water, and a great attitude. Balls are provided.
Can I cancel my membership?
Yes, anytime through the customer portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your billing month.
